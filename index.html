How this map works
  • Every line rests on a source we checked by hand, with the exact sentence quoted. Where a company's own page says it, we say so; where only press reports say it, we say that too.
  • "Who pays for it" means who each agent, service or payment network makes its own money from. It is not the whole company behind it, and not what you pay for the things an agent buys for you. We draw it only when a source says so.
  • We describe, we don't label. The quotes carry the weight.
  • Every change lives in the public history of this site. We correct with new entries, never by rewriting old ones.
  • Companies on this map can send corrections through our About page.
  • Not on the map doesn't mean it doesn't exist.
  • The whole dataset is one file: data/map.json. Reuse it under CC BY 4.0: credit First Frost and link the map. Quotes belong to their authors.

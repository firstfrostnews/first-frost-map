# First Frost · Agent Map

Who pays the AI agents that shop for you, who lets them in, who turns them away, and who carries the risk when something goes wrong. Live at [map.firstfrost.news](https://map.firstfrost.news), from [First Frost](https://firstfrost.news).

## The data

Everything on the map comes from one file, [`data/map.json`](data/map.json). Each line on the map is one claim in that file: who, what relationship, with whom, a plain sentence, the exact quote it rests on, a link to the source, an archived copy where we could make one, and the date we checked it.

## Rules

1. Every line has a source. We quote the sentence, link the page and say whether it is the company's own words or a news report.
2. We describe, we don't label. The quotes carry the weight.
3. We never rewrite history. A correction is a new commit that says what changed and why.
4. Companies on the map can send corrections through [our About page](https://firstfrost.news/about).
5. Not on the map doesn't mean it doesn't exist. What we looked for and didn't find is listed in the file.
